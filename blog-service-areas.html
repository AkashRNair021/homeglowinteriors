Service Locations | June 16, 2026

At Home Glow Interiors & Builders, we are proud to offer our interior design, renovation, landscaping, modular kitchen, custom furniture, gypsum ceiling, and construction-related services across various locations in Kerala. Our goal is to bring creative design solutions, quality workmanship, and professional project management closer to homeowners and businesses throughout the region.

Over the years, we have successfully worked on projects in Pathanamthitta, Adoor, Pandalam, Thiruvalla, Chengannur, Kollam, Kottarakkara, Anchal, Karunagappally, Kayamkulam, Mavelikkara, Haripad, and surrounding areas. Each project is approached with the same commitment to quality, functionality, and attention to detail that defines the Home Glow experience.

Whether you are planning a new home, renovating an existing property, upgrading your interiors, designing a modular kitchen, or creating a beautiful outdoor landscape, our team is ready to assist you. We continue to expand our services to meet the growing needs of clients across Kerala while maintaining our focus on personalized solutions and customer satisfaction.
