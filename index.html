Our Process

Simple & Hassle-Free Process

We follow a structured 6-step process to ensure transparency, quality, and complete customer satisfaction.

01

Consultation & Requirement

Discussing your vision, goals, and budget to draft the ideal concept path.

02

Site Visit & Measurements

Conducting detailed site inspections and taking precise physical measurements.

03

2D Planning & 3D Visual

Creating layouts and realistic 3D renderings to preview spaces before building.

04

Design Approval

Finalizing specifications, custom finishes, colors, and styling layouts.

05

Execution & Installation

Executing designs with premium quality craftsmanship and strict project oversight.

06

Final Handover

Thorough quality inspections followed by clean handover and walkthrough.
